Search behaviour is shifting quickly: AI Overviews now appear on roughly 48% of tracked U.S. queries, and where they appear they cut the click-through rate of organic results by almost 60%. That is not a reason to give up on rankings. It is a reason to compete for a second kind of visibility.

Brands cited in AI Overviews see 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks than brands not cited on the same query, and traffic arriving from AI referrals converts at 4.4× the rate of standard organic traffic.

The catch is that ranking is no guarantee of being cited. Independent analysis found the share of AI Overview citations coming from pages that also rank in the organic top ten fell from 76% to 38% in about eighteen months. Rankings and citations are becoming two separate battles, so we treat them as two separate, connected, deliverables.
