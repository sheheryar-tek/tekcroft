The precision matters here because Google frequently overrides what’s on the page: it rewrites meta descriptions 62.78% of the time and title tags 33.4% of the time when they’re poorly matched to the query or too long. 25% of top-ranking pages don’t even have a written meta description (Ahrefs, 2026); pages with duplicate or missing metadata are exactly the kind of pages that get skipped when Google auto-generates a snippet, or when an AI answer engine decides your page isn’t a clean enough source to cite.
