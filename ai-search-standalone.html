Is Technical SEO Still Relevant in the Age of AI Search?

It is more important now. ChatGPT, Perplexity, or AI Overviews from Google will not magically understand your site. Most, if not all of them, will use a process referred to as retrieval-augmented generation. In short, the AI will actively pull live pages, read the code, extract facts, and then respond to queries. This means the same basic technical SEO that gets you crawled and ranked by Google will determine whether an AI engine can find you, read you, or even cite you.

Left out of AI answers

AI crawlers hit many of the same walls that traditional search engines historically have. Googlebot can't render your JS? Broken schema? Hidden pages that require 3 or more clicks to access? Any of these, and it means you'll be missed by AI search technology.
