How We Prevent Duplicate Content Across Franchise Location Pages 
Duplicate, thin location pages are the single most common technical failure in franchise SEO; these are among the easiest for Google to devalue. Here's how we prevent it:
Unique content requirements per location: service mix, coverage area, staff bios, local FAQs, not a copy-pasted paragraph with the city name changed.
Clear canonical rules: so near-identical territory or service-area pages don't compete against the page actually meant to rank.
A content governance system: rules for what corporate writes, what local teams can adjust, or what needs approval before publishing.
Internal linking discipline: every location page links up to its city/state hub and back to the national brand, instead of existing in isolation.
The result: each location page earns its own relevance signal, instead of diluting the network's collective authority.
