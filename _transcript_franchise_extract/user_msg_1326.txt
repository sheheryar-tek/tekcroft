What is the difference between franchise seo and local seo? 
Local SEO focuses on a single business, while Franchise SEO requires focusing on all locations of a brand. Additionally, there are other considerations, like making sure all locations use the same brand elements and avoiding duplicate content.

How much does franchise SEO cost? 
The price of franchise SEO has several factors, including the overall size of the franchise, how competitive the market is, the amount of technical and content requirements for each location, and many other considerations. In general, smaller franchises can expect to pay a few thousand dollars a month, whereas larger franchises may pay in the tens of thousands of dollars a month.
