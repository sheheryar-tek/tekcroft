Franchise SEO explained 
 What Is Franchise SEO? 

SEO for Franchises means optimizing search results for brands operating through multiple
 locations or franchisees. It works on two levels at once:

National brand authority: the corporate domain’s overall trust and visibility.

Local, location-level visibility: we can analyze each franchise unit individually, whether it’s a
 specific city or neighborhood, or even the Google Maps Pack.

This is not the same as traditional local SEO , which only has to optimize for a single location, or
 enterprise SEO , which is focused on large, single website domains. Franchise SEO comes with unique
 challenges, including the potential for duplicate content across multiple websites , and the need for
 extensive reporting at the unit level .
