It is one integrated approach — profile optimisation, NAP citations, location-based content and review management together — rather than four separate solutions.

Why Some Competitors Outrank You With Fewer Reviews

Reviews matter, but they are one piece of the puzzle rather than the whole algorithm. A competitor can sit below you on reviews and still rank above you if their GBP category is more relevant, their citations more consistent, and their page content a closer answer to what the searcher actually asked.

It is the most consistent finding in our audits: businesses with 80 reviews sitting below competitors with 30, because those competitors have the listing, the content and the citations fully in order.
