Our process

Our Software Development Services Process

Five phases, and two of them happen before a single line of code is written. More than half of all projects suffer from scope creep; the first step is designed to avoid it.

The 5-step process 0102030405 of five stages

01Discovery & Requirements

Your workflows, pain points and business objectives are mapped before design begins. Scope is set in this process, so it doesn’t change mid-project.

02Design & Architecture

Architects design the technical architecture, database and integrations before coding commences. You approve the full blueprint before developers write any code.

03Agile Development

Developers build in short sprints, so functional software is delivered every two weeks. Priorities can change between sprints without ruining the delivery schedule.

04Testing & QA

Every feature is tested for functionality, security and performance before it comes to you. QA runs alongside development, never as a rushed final step.

05Launch & Ongoing Support

We handle the launch and carry on with monitoring, support and new feature work. Launching is a significant moment, but not the end of the engagement.

Pricing guide

Cost of Hiring a Mobile App Development Agency

Here’s a useful range instead of making you fill out a form to find out you’re outside your budget.

TierTypical rangeWhat’s included
  • Simple MVP $10,000 – $30,000 Single platform, core features, minimal backend
  • Mid-Complexity App $30,000 – $80,000 API integrations, custom features, cross-platform
  • Enterprise-Grade App $80,000 – $400,000+ AI/ML, complex integrations, compliance requirements

Where the budget changes:

  • Don’t compare development quotes on the headline number alone. Ask what’s included. A second platform, payment integration, additional user roles, or compliance requirements can move the budget substantially.

  • You should know who will be working on the app development.

  • The average custom app costs around $171,450, but most small-to-mid business apps land between $50,000–$120,000. If a quote you’re getting elsewhere seems dramatically outside that range in either direction, ask why.

  • Annual maintenance typically runs 15–20% of the original build cost. Factor that into your first-year budget, along with your launch budget.

The single most useful thing you can bring us is a screen-by-screen breakdown of what your app needs to do, written as if you had to explain it to someone with zero context. It needs to be specific, because it makes the quote you get comparable.

We wouldn’t recommend building two native apps just because you eventually want to be on both stores. An MVP is often the better starting point. It puts the core product in users’ hands sooner and gives you evidence about what people actually use before you spend on the full feature set.
