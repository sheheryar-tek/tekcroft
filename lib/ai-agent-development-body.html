Our process

Our AI Chatbot Development Process

Our chatbot developers follow a five-stage development process designed for accountability, not guesswork. This is where most chatbot app development projects succeed or fail.

The 5-stage process 0102030405 of five stages

01Discovery and Requirements Mapping

We map your systems, workflows, and edge cases before starting the development process. This helps avoid scope changes that usually ruin most projects.

02Conversation Design and Escalation Logic

Our dialogue flow, memory stack, fallback system, and escalation triggers all work as a single unit. 81% of customers want service to flow continuously without any need for repetition. Full context is transferred once your chatbot completes the conversation.

03Development, RAG Setup and Model Integration

We develop the chatbot, connect to your knowledge base, and connect to your selected model. Integration is done with testing on real data rather than scripts.

04Testing, QA and Deployment

We test against real conversations, edge cases, and adversarial prompts before going live. Monitoring begins with the first real conversation, not afterwards.

05Ongoing Monitoring, Retraining and Maintenance

We measure performance and keep retraining your chatbot according to any changes within your business or data. This alone solves most issues around accuracy.

An AI agent plans, makes decisions, and performs multi-step actions through your tools and data. A chatbot only answers within a specific conversation thread. An AI agent uses tools, connects to systems, and performs tasks with minimal human intervention.

Agentic AI development refers to designing systems that can reason, plan, and act autonomously. Agents make their own decisions and react to changing information. They carry out multi-stage processes by coordinating across different tools, APIs, and corporate data with little supervision required.

Costs depend on agent complexity, the number of integrations, and the number of workflows to be executed. The cost for task-level agents is much lower compared to enterprise-level multi-agent systems. We provide transparent, exact quotations after a short discovery call. No hidden charges, no vague estimates.

An AI agent developer is responsible for designing, developing, integrating, and testing the AI agents that function autonomously within your organization’s infrastructure. This encompasses the whole process from planning and development through testing and maintenance to ensure their continued accuracy as your processes change.
