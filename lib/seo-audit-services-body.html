What we do

What a Tekcroft SEO Audit Covers

Explore all services ↑

No two sites fail in the same way, so no two audits look the same. We work through six areas in every engagement and go deep wherever the data says the real problem is — then tell you which findings are worth your time and which are noise.

Most invisible pages are invisible for a technical reason. We crawl the site the way a search engine does and record every barrier standing between your pages and the index.

  • Crawl budget waste and crawl errors
  • Indexation and Search Console coverage gaps
  • robots.txt and XML sitemap accuracy
  • Canonical tags and duplicate URLs
  • Redirect chains, loops and broken links
  • Core Web Vitals and page speed
  • Mobile rendering and usability
  • JavaScript rendering and hydration issues
  • HTTPS, security headers and hreflang setup
  • Structured data validation
Learn more

Ranking is not only about what a page contains, but about whether it answers the query better than the pages already sitting above it. We review the pages that matter and the ones that should.

  • Title tags and meta descriptions
  • Heading structure and on-page hierarchy
  • Keyword targeting and cannibalisation
  • Thin, duplicate and outdated content
  • Content gaps against the pages already ranking
  • Image alt text and media optimisation
  • Search intent alignment page by page
  • Content decay and refresh priorities
Learn more

How a site is organised decides what gets crawled, what inherits authority and what is never found at all. We map the whole structure, not just the top two levels.

  • URL structure and folder depth
  • Navigation and menu logic
  • Orphan pages and dead ends
  • Click depth from the homepage
  • Internal link distribution and anchor text
  • Breadcrumbs and pagination handling
  • Faceted navigation and filter URLs
  • Sitemap-to-structure alignment
Learn more

Links still carry authority — and risk. We look at what is pointing at the site, what it is actually worth, and what may be doing quiet damage.

  • Referring domains and link quality
  • Anchor text distribution and over-optimisation
  • Toxic and spam link identification
  • Lost, broken and redirected backlinks
  • Link gaps against competing sites
  • Internal versus external authority flow
  • Disavow recommendations where they are warranted
Learn more

An audit that only looks inward misses half the picture. We benchmark the site against the ones already winning the searches you want.

  • Visibility benchmarking against direct competitors
  • Keywords they rank for and you do not
  • SERP feature ownership and lost real estate
  • Content depth and format comparison
  • Technical and speed comparison
  • Opportunity sizing by volume and intent
Learn more

If the measurement is wrong, every decision made after it is wrong too. We verify that what you are reporting is what is actually happening.

  • Google Analytics 4 configuration
  • Search Console setup and property coverage
  • Goal and conversion tracking accuracy
  • Event and ecommerce tracking integrity
  • Filters, bot traffic and data quality
  • Attribution and channel grouping
  • Reporting views your team will actually open
