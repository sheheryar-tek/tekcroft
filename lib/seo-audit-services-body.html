What we do

What Our SEO Audit Services Cover

Explore all services ↑

Every audit works through six different levels: technical, content, backlinks, competitors, AI search visibility, and analytics accuracy. Our seo site audit services go beyond a surface scan into manual, prioritized review.

Technical SEO Audit

Our technical SEO audit services check crawlability to ensure Google can actually find your pages. We test indexation to make sure only the correct pages end up in Google’s index. We run a full site speed audit alongside Core Web Vitals and JavaScript Rendering, both of which hardly ever become clear from tool exports but cause eligibility problems all the time.

On-Page & Content Audit

As part of our content audit services, we audit metadata and header structure with regard to search intent match. We highlight keyword cannibalization, where several pages compete for the same keyword instead of one page winning clearly. We identify decaying content, pages that once ranked but have slowly faded away unnoticed.

Backlink & Authority Audit

Our backlink audit services analyze your backlink profile to identify any toxic or spammy backlinks that may be damaging your authority score. We measure your authority gap relative to your real competitors you’re actually losing rankings to, not a generic industry benchmark.

Competitor Gap Analysis

We run a keyword gap analysis and content gap analysis at the keyword level to see exactly where your competitors are outranking you and why, whether it’s through content depth, technical superiority, or backlinking.

AI Search (GEO) Readiness Audit

We check whether ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google’s AI Overviews can discover, interpret, and cite your website. It’s more critical than most audits realize: 65% of US adults are reading AI-generated summaries in their search results, and websites that aren’t optimized for this layer are getting lost in it completely.

Analytics & Conversion Tracking Audit

We verify that your GA4 and conversion data are reliable before giving any recommendations. Sites that we audit have been basing their decisions on inaccurate data; all their past “insights” have been wrong from the start.

Google’s Search Central guidelines explicitly state that solving crawl and indexing problems comes first, as the cornerstone of SEO. That is precisely why we begin with this layer before content or backlinks ever enter the conversation.
