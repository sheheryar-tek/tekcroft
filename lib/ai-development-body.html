Our process

How We Build: From Idea To Production

Every engagement goes through the same five steps tailored to your project requirements.

The 5-stage process 0102030405 of five stages

Scope the Problem, Not the Technology

The business result and success criteria are defined before the model selection.

Data & Feasibility Audit

Your current data is validated in terms of completeness, quality, and availability before the project.

Proof of Concept

We validate our approach based on the real data upfront. Issues are caught early and are inexpensive to solve.

Production Build & Integration

The system is built against your actual infrastructure and access rules, not against a sandbox environment that behaves differently down the road.

Deploy, Monitor & Govern Cost

Model accuracy, drift, and spend are monitored post-launch. Monitoring doesn’t end with the deployment.

Custom AI Development vs. Fine-Tuningvs. Off-the-Shelf APIs

Not every project needs a custom build. Here’s how to know which one yours does.

Approach Best Fit Why
Custom AI development Core, proprietary use cases built on your own data and workflows Off-the-shelf tools can’t replicate logic unique to how your business runs
Fine Tuning A general model performs well but needs consistent, domain-specific output It aligns an existing model to your data without a full custom build
Off-the-shelf APIs Speed matters more than deep customization A general model already solves the core problem without added cost

Where AI Integration Projects Actually Break

Most AI projects don't fail at the model. They fail where the system meets your real infrastructure, permissions, and traffic.

  1. Unprepared data

    Disparate or untagged records get audited before building begins, not found halfway into the project.

  2. Pilot projects that never reach production

    Each build is run against real-world systems and real-world loads, not sample data.

  3. Writeback issues

    Any write to your CRM or database gets tracked and rolled back should it fail partway through.

  4. Latency under load

    Latency is tested using your real traffic, not a quiet environment designed for demos.

  5. Uncontrolled costs post-launch

    Usage costs and retries are limited and watched from day one, not open-ended.

  6. Model version drift

    Model versions are locked and validated before any provider updates your system.

  7. Security issues around access

    Retrieval adheres to your permissions, so nothing can be returned that a user is not authorized to view.

An AI development service develops and implements custom AI solutions, such as machine learning models, generative AI, and automation, tailored to specific business needs, rather than a general-purpose software product.

The software development process involves writing out certain rules that dictate the way in which an output is generated every single time. The AI development process, however, involves building systems that learn from data patterns and become better with more and more data.

The cost of custom AI development depends on the scope of work, data availability, system integrations, and model choice, so there’s no single rate. A chatbot project costs much less than a multi-agent system integrated with CRM and ERP.

Timeline depends on data preparedness and number of integrations rather than the size of the project. One system proof of concept takes the least amount of time, while multi-system builds take longer due to testing and access setup.
