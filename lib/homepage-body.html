The new search reality

Your Customers Aren’t Just Searching Google Anymore.

Search has split into two channels. Google still handles the large majority of queries, but AI Overviews now appear on roughly half of U.S. Google searches, up from about one in five just a year earlier. Alongside that shift, ChatGPT reports more than 900 million weekly users, and Google’s own Gemini has grown past 400 million. Businesses optimizing only for traditional blue-link rankings are optimizing for half the picture.

Tekcroft builds visibility for both.

That means structuring content so it can be cited inside an AI Overview or a ChatGPT answer, not just ranked on a results page, and tracking where your brand appears across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity the same way we track keyword rankings.
