What Is Google Business Profile SEO & Why It Matters

Your Google Business Profile is often where a local customer decides who to call or visit. They can compare you against competitors in seconds — and 76% of local searches lead to a visit or call within 24 hours.

01

What Google looks at

Local Google Maps ranking factors come down to three things: relevance, distance, and prominence. You cannot control distance, but Maps optimisation can improve relevance and prominence.

02

Small gaps can cost the call

Wrong categories, thin photos or stale reviews weaken a profile — even when you technically outrank the business that gets chosen.

03

GBP now goes beyond Maps

AI Overviews appear on roughly 40% of local search queries, and people increasingly ask ChatGPT and Gemini for local recommendations. Your GBP feeds those answers too.

GBP Optimization Is Notthe Same for Every Industry

Tekcroft understands your industry. The work is the same nine pieces everywhere — what changes is which signals matter most, and what we put first.

01

Home Services

HVAC, plumbing and electrical have the highest immediate-call rate of any vertical, so we optimise for the call button and service-area accuracy over everything else — these searches rarely browse a website first.

 02

Healthcare & Dental

Review thresholds are highest here: patients typically expect a median of 40+ reviews and 4.5+ stars before booking. We prioritise review volume and response consistency, plus HIPAA-safe Q&A handling.

 03

Legal & Financial Services

Lowest local-pack click-through of any vertical, which means every click matters more. We focus on trust signals: credentials, specific practice-area categories and detailed service descriptions.

Highest local-pack engagement. Photo volume and Google Posts are the cheapest lever here — menu photos, daily specials and event posts consistently outperform paid promotion.

 05

Real Estate

Multiple agents often share one brokerage address, which creates NAP and category conflicts. We structure multi-agent listings so individual agents don’t cannibalise each other’s visibility.

Multi-Location & Franchise Brands

Bulk NAP management, brand-consistent descriptions with location-specific service details, and centralised reporting across every location — with local relevance kept intact.

Know the difference

Which One Is Best for GBP Optimization:DIY or Professional Service

If you’re a single-location business in a market with light competition, you can genuinely do a lot of this yourself. The Google dashboard is free, and our services section above works as a checklist.

Professional management is about consistency, because profiles that go quiet after the first month lose the ranking gains they made. If you don’t have a standing weekly slot for this, that’s the actual problem we solve.
