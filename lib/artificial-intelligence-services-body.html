FAQs

Artificial Intelligence services focus on the design, development, and deployment of AI applications for businesses. They can include custom models, AI chatbots, or software agents.

Concept clearance takes a few weeks. A full production system takes longer, depending on data readiness, integration complexity, or scope. Our AI specialists can give you a detailed timeline after the initial scoping stage.

We develop and integrate custom AI models, like chatbots and software agents; automate and improve business processes through rule based predictive systems. We can also develop an AI system to extract and analyze data contained in various business documentation.

A chatbot is ideal for applications that primarily involve interaction or providing information, while an AI agent is for applications that involve interaction, as well as facilitating action. Mostly, an AI chatbot is the starting point and foundation for an AI agent.

Your expected use and data define how much you will be charged. Integrating a solution for the first time is usually less costly compared to a full scale production. The best way to get an exact figure is a short scoping call.

AI development companies offer end to end services for AI solutions, including analysis of the available data, then working on architecture and deployment. The integration of AI solutions with the client’s system, along with ongoing support and maintenance, also falls under the scope of AI agencies.

Prior to the deployment of an AI solution, we evaluate different types of data and edge cases to define performance metrics. We also define monitoring mechanisms to evaluate the post deployment performance of the AI solution.

Outsourcing makes sense when you need results quickly, don’t have AI specialists on staff, or want to validate an idea before committing to a permanent team. Building in house tends to make sense only when AI is core to your product, or when you can fund a longer ramp up. A hybrid approach, like an external partner ships the first system while your team learns alongside it, is very common.
