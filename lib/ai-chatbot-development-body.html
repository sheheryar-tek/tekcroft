Our process

Our AI Chatbot Development Process

Our chatbot developers follow a five-stage development process designed for accountability, not guesswork. This is where most chatbot app development projects succeed or fail.

The 5-stage process 0102030405 of five stages

Discovery and Requirements Mapping

We map your systems, workflows, and edge cases before starting the development process. This helps avoid scope changes that usually ruin most projects.

Conversation Design and Escalation Logic

Our dialogue flow, memory stack, fallback system, and escalation triggers all work as a single unit. 81% of customers want service to flow continuously without any need for repetition. Full context is transferred once your chatbot completes the conversation.

Development, RAG Setup and Model Integration

We develop the chatbot, connect to your knowledge base, and connect to your selected model. Integration is done with testing on real data rather than scripts.

Testing, QA and Deployment

We test against real conversations, edge cases, and adversarial prompts before going live. Monitoring begins with the first real conversation, not afterwards.

Ongoing Monitoring, Retraining and Maintenance

We measure performance and keep retraining your chatbot according to any changes within your business or data. This alone solves most issues around accuracy.

The cost of building an AI chatbot is determined by its scope, integration, and compliance requirements; therefore, the cost varies greatly from one project to another. An FAQ chatbot costs much less than a corporate assistant integrated with a CRM or billing system. We determine the scope of each project after a brief discovery session.

You can create your own chatbot without coding, but that’s only enough for basic FAQ chats. The chatbot stops working if there is a need for proper integrations and security measures. Custom programming is justified if your chatbot requires interaction with real business data. We’ll be honest about the best choice for you.

The most effective builder depends on the requirements of your business rather than using a solution that works for everyone. The no-code platforms are quick and inexpensive for simple cases. Custom-made chatbots become effective where there is a need for integrations, compliance, and multilingual capabilities. We help you to compare both before recommending one.

Outdated answers are usually generated by a database that is not updated once the chatbot is launched. Monitoring and updating processes are part of our workflow from day one. Your chatbot always stays up to date with your documents, prices, and policies. It’s a built-in step, not an afterthought.

It seamlessly passes control to a human agent right away, with the entire conversation history carried forward. There’s no need for the customer to re-explain anything. This is more important than many vendors realize. 87% of customers consider human access crucial in such situations.

Timelines depend on scale. The timeline for a proof of concept is shorter than an enterprise build with multiple integrations. We develop a realistic timeline during the discovery phase before signing a contract. You’ll have the exact delivery dates right from the start, with no uncertain promises in between.

The processing of your data is governed by US-compliant protocols such as HIPAA for healthcare information and CCPA for consumer protection. We also ensure compliance with SOC 2 controls where applicable. Ownership of the data remains yours after the project ends.

We provide maintenance services, which include monitoring and training after the launch of the product. Chatbots that are not maintained quickly become outdated in a few weeks due to changes in your business. We make sure your knowledge base stays up-to-date, and your escalation is functioning correctly.
