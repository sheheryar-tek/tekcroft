Our process

How Our AI SEO Engagement Works

Each engagement goes through five phases with a concrete deliverable at the end of each one. We don't build anything before we establish a baseline of how your brand currently stands in AI search.

The 5-phase process 0102030405 of five phases

Prompt Discovery & AI Visibility Baseline (Weeks 1-2)

Through AI keyword research, we develop 20-50 buyer prompts and test every one on ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and AI Overviews. You receive a baseline AI visibility report.

Source Influence Mapping (Weeks 3-4)

We identify the sources powering AI responses and align each high-priority prompt with one of your web pages. We turn your AI SEO strategy into a prioritized 90-day action plan.

On-Site Citation Optimization (Month 2)

We use AI content optimization for priority pages so that the answer comes first. Schema, entity, and AI crawler-access fixes ship with the content updates.

Third-Party Source Outreach (Months 2-3)

Our generative engine optimization extends off-site, earning mentions on the sites and publications AI already trusts. Outdated brand descriptions get corrected, and every placement is documented.

Recommendation Tracking & Iteration (Ongoing)

Every prompt is re-tested monthly, including the follow-up suggestions AI gives buyers. You get a monthly AI visibility report and a quarterly strategy review.
